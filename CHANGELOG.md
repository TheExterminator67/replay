# Changelog

## 1.1.0

The big one.

- **Player:** crossfade (0–10 s), A–B loop, speed slider 0.5×–1.5×, EQ presets (Bass, Vocal, Phonk, Night), waveform progress bar, smart shuffle
- **Library:** select many sounds at once (play, queue, like, tag, move, remove) and Undo
- **Find the full song** on Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube or SoundCloud
- **More like this:** same sound, same creator, shared tags, other TikToks with the sound
- **Share a single sound** with a link that plays in the browser, and **story cards** for Instagram / TikTok / Snapchat
- **Public profiles** (`/@you`), **collaborative playlists** and **Listen together** live rooms
- **Save to Replay** browser extension for Chrome / Edge (`Replay-extension-1.1.0.zip`)
- **Arabic** (العربية) with a right-to-left layout
- Playlist photos stored in the cloud when signed in
- Privacy policy and terms; optional anonymous usage stats and crash reports (Settings → Privacy)

## 1.0.0

First release of Replay for Mac and Windows.

- Save sounds from TikTok links (one, many at once, drag & drop, or a creator's profile)
- Playlists with photos, pinning, tags, sorting and search
- Background playback, media keys, queue, shuffle/repeat, Sped up / Slowed, sleep timer
- Full-screen player with visualizer, themes and cover-colored tint
- Stats and listening streaks
- Optional accounts (Google or email) with sync and shareable playlists
