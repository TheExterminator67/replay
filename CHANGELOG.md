# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release of Replay for Mac and Windows.

- Save sounds from TikTok links (one, many at once, drag & drop, or a creator's profile)
- Playlists with photos, pinning, tags, sorting and search
- Background playback, media keys, queue, shuffle/repeat, Sped up / Slowed, sleep timer
- Full-screen player with visualizer, themes and cover-colored tint
- Stats and listening streaks
- Optional accounts (Google or email) with sync and shareable playlists
